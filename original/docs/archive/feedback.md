 ● V4 review verdict                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   V4 is substantially better, but I still would not sign off the handoff as “build complete.” Most original ledger defects are genuinely fixed. However, the validation exit gate is broken, payment-status semantics are wrong, and several handoff claims remain overstated.                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Confirmed improvements                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Independent Parquet checks confirm:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • Statements reconcile to purchases and refunds with effectively zero floating-point delta.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
   • Zero-activity statements with phantom activity: 0.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
   • lifetime_purchases now reconciles exactly to approved transactions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
   • Final-payment flushing works: 11,203 accounts have $41.76M of payments after their final statement, and current balances reconcile.                                                                                                                                                                                                               
   • Pre-issue, post-replacement, and post-churn transactions: 0.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
   • Five charge-offs all have close dates.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
   • card_purchase now preserves refund signs and REFUND_CONFIRMED statuses.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
   • Seeded raw UUIDs, declared dependencies, corrected privacy wording, and improved causal framing are all real improvements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
   • Inventory arithmetic is correct: 27,304,639 rows, approximately 1,794 MiB.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Blocking findings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   1. Critical: validation failures still cannot fail the build                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   LAST_FAIL_COUNT is initialized at build/validation.py:18 but never incremented by Report.check() at build/validation.py:25-33. Therefore build/run_all.py:168-194 always sees zero failures.                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   This already happened: the v4 build log (deleted from the repo; see git history), line 91 records a delinquency FAIL, but the build continues through completion at the v4 build log (deleted from the repo; see git history), lines 122-125.                                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   The claims at docs/archive/HANDOFF_FOR_REVIEW.md:27, :189, and :420 that the build exits 1 on any FAIL are currently false.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   2. High: the payment waterfall does not support the handoff’s claims                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   The new allocation bridge is useful, but it excludes opening debt from billed_amount and ignores unallocated payment remainders.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • PAID_FULL compares payments with current-cycle billed charges rather than closing_balance (build/facts.py:1333-1340).                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
   • 199,700 statements marked PAID_FULL have not paid their closing balance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
   • 53,028 payments are not fully allocated, representing $170.32M; 2,592 payments have no allocation rows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
   • 7,776 positive-balance statements are allocated above closing balance, contradicting the claimed zero over-allocation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
   • The over-allocation validator uses max(billed_amount, minimum_due) instead of closing balance (build/validation.py:271-272).                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
   • No validator checks that allocations sum back to each payment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   is_late is also derived from the last allocation date, not whether the required minimum was satisfied by the due date (build/facts.py:1347):                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • 132,878 statements are marked late despite the minimum being paid on time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
   • 6,491 statements are not marked late despite an unpaid minimum after the due date.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   The handoff statement that paid_amount, payment_status, and is_late are “provably consistent” is therefore inaccurate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   3. High: delinquent installment plans remain ACTIVE                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Only LATE, not overdue PARTIAL, sets the delinquency flag (build/facts.py:1367-1382).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • 13,269 expired plans remain ACTIVE despite overdue partial payments and outstanding balances.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
   • Every one has due-unpaid amounts and delinquent schedule rows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
   • The new installment validators do not test this classification rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   4. High: raw sign/type repair is incomplete                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   card_purchase is fixed, but card_voucher_qr_code is not:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • All 2,922 sampled QR refunds remain positive and marked COMPLETED.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
   • The blanket raw-mirror claim at docs/archive/HANDOFF_FOR_REVIEW.md:295-299 needs narrowing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Raw card identity is also unreliable:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • 149,976 of 150,000 raw purchases have cardLast4 inconsistent with the transaction’s card; it is randomly regenerated at build/raw_mirror.py:265.                                                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Remaining issues that should be disclosed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • 60,067 transactions occur after card expiry, across 650 cards. Issue and replacement dates are now enforced, but expiry is not.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
   • 6,297 refunds still have no approved source purchase; the pre-decline refund-mask bug remains.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
   • 209,270 transactions have international flags inconsistent with merchant geography.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
   • Five ordinary payments totaling $100,482.52 occur after charge-off closure, without recovery or reinstatement classification, despite the stated absence of recovery cash flows.                                                                                                                                                                  
   • Independent DPD reconstruction found one residual snapshot with a $0.01 past-due difference and six-day DPD mismatch. Immaterial financially, but the claimed zero-mismatch result is not exact.                                                                                                                                                  
   • Statement/snapshot credit limits still disagree on all 2,233 limit-event dates.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Handoff corrections needed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   • Actual snapshot delinquency shares are 88.96% CURRENT and 9.45% 1–29, not approximately 90.1% and 8.4% as stated at docs/archive/HANDOFF_FOR_REVIEW.md:270-276.                                                                                                                                                                                                
   • The document title says “v2”, although it is the v4 handoff.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
   • “386 vendors” should be 386 mapping rows covering 385 distinct source vendor names.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
   • Three reconciliation deltas are blank in validation_report.md:78-81 because the detail strings are literally "max delta ".                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
   • The claimed exact-cent validator still uses $1.01 tolerances at build/validation.py:238-270.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
   • The data dictionary describes statement payment_status as POSTED / RETURNED / PENDING, and allocation amount_usd as a signed transaction amount.                                                                                                                                                                                                  
   • README.md omits the allocation fact from its schema section and points to the wrong causal section.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Recommended disposition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   Keep v4 as a release candidate, not an accepted build. Fix the fail counter, redefine the payment waterfall around closing balances and unapplied cash, correct lateness and plan statuses, then rerun validation and update the handoff with actual results.                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   The causal and privacy sections are now notably more honest and can largely be retained.
